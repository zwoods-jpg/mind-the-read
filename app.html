Mind The Read
No-limit hold'em trainer

Every other poker trainer asks if you know the right answer. They deal you an isolated spot with no history, no opponent, no session — flashcards with beautiful math behind them. But nobody loses money because they missed an equilibrium frequency on the turn. They lose because they can't read the player across the table and adjust.

This trains you to play.

  • A real table — full hands, real betting, position, blinds, showdowns
  • Opponents with identities — the nit, the station, the maniac — each with leaks you have to discover
  • A coach that grades every decision, against the book and against the player actually sitting there
  • A campaign that starts at "what beats what" and builds, one skill at a time

Nobody is born knowing this. Start at level one. The rest is practice.
